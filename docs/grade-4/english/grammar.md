# Grammaire anglaise

## Les pronoms personnels

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| **I** | je |
| **you** | tu / vous |
| **he** | il |
| **she** | elle |
| **it** | il/elle (chose, animal) |
| **we** | nous |
| **they** | ils / elles |

!!! tip "A retenir"
    En anglais, **I** (je) s'ecrit toujours avec une majuscule !

---

## Le verbe BE (etre)

### Conjugaison au present

| Pronom | Be | Exemple |
|--------|-----|---------|
| I | **am** | I am a student. |
| You | **are** | You are nice. |
| He/She/It | **is** | She is happy. |
| We | **are** | We are friends. |
| They | **are** | They are at school. |

### Forme contractee

| Forme longue | Forme contractee |
|--------------|------------------|
| I am | I'm |
| You are | You're |
| He is | He's |
| She is | She's |
| It is | It's |
| We are | We're |
| They are | They're |

### Forme negative

| Affirmatif | Negatif |
|------------|---------|
| I am | I am not / I'm not |
| He is | He is not / He isn't |
| They are | They are not / They aren't |

---

## Le verbe HAVE (avoir)

### Conjugaison au present

| Pronom | Have | Exemple |
|--------|------|---------|
| I | **have** | I have a dog. |
| You | **have** | You have a book. |
| He/She/It | **has** | She has blue eyes. |
| We | **have** | We have a big house. |
| They | **have** | They have three cats. |

!!! warning "Attention"
    He, she, it → **has** (pas have !)

---

## Le present simple

### Formation

| Pronom | Verbe | Exemple |
|--------|-------|---------|
| I / You / We / They | verbe | I play football. |
| He / She / It | verbe + **s** | He plays football. |

### Exemples

| Francais | Anglais |
|----------|---------|
| Je mange une pomme | I eat an apple |
| Elle mange une pomme | She eat**s** an apple |
| Ils jouent au foot | They play football |
| Il joue au foot | He play**s** football |

!!! tip "Regle du S"
    A la 3e personne du singulier (he, she, it), on ajoute **-s** au verbe.

---

## Poser des questions

### Questions avec BE

| Affirmatif | Question |
|------------|----------|
| You are happy | **Are** you happy? |
| He is at home | **Is** he at home? |

On inverse le sujet et le verbe !

### Questions avec DO/DOES

| Affirmatif | Question |
|------------|----------|
| You like pizza | **Do** you like pizza? |
| She likes pizza | **Does** she like pizza? |

!!! warning "Attention"
    Avec **does** (he, she, it), le verbe perd son **-s** !
    She like**s** → Does she like?

### Mots interrogatifs

| Anglais | Francais | Exemple |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **What** | Quoi/Que | What is your name? |
| **Where** | Ou | Where do you live? |
| **When** | Quand | When is your birthday? |
| **Who** | Qui | Who is your teacher? |
| **How** | Comment | How are you? |
| **How old** | Quel age | How old are you? |

## Les articles

| Article | Utilisation | Exemple |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **a** | devant consonne | a book, a dog |
| **an** | devant voyelle | an apple, an elephant |
| **the** | specifique | the teacher, the cat |

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Conjugue BE : She ___ happy"

??? success "Correction"
    She **is** happy

!!! question "Q2. Conjugue au present : He (play) tennis"

??? success "Correction"
    He **plays** tennis (3e personne → +s)

!!! question "Q3. Mets a la forme interrogative : You like chocolate"

??? success "Correction"
    **Do** you like chocolate?

!!! question "Q4. Complete avec a ou an : ___ orange, ___ book"

??? success "Correction"
    **an** orange, **a** book
