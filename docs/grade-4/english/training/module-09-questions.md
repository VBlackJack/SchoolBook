# Module 9 - Poser des questions

!!! abstract "Objectifs"
    - Poser des questions avec BE
    - Poser des questions avec DO/DOES
    - Connaitre les mots interrogatifs

    **Duree** : 1 heure

---

## Questions avec BE

### Formation

On **inverse** le sujet et le verbe BE :

| Affirmatif | Question |
|------------|----------|
| You **are** happy | **Are** you happy? |
| He **is** at home | **Is** he at home? |
| They **are** French | **Are** they French? |

### Reponses courtes

| Question | Oui | Non |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Are you happy? | Yes, I **am**. | No, I**'m not**. |
| Is he at school? | Yes, he **is**. | No, he **isn't**. |
| Are they French? | Yes, they **are**. | No, they **aren't**. |

---

## Questions avec DO/DOES

### Formation

On utilise **DO** ou **DOES** au debut :

| Affirmatif | Question |
|------------|----------|
| You like pizza | **Do** you like pizza? |
| She plays tennis | **Does** she play tennis? |

!!! warning "Attention"
    Avec **does**, le verbe perd son **-s** !
    - She play**s** → Does she play?

### Quand utiliser DO ou DOES ?

| Pronom | Auxiliaire |
|--------|------------|
| I, You, We, They | **DO** |
| He, She, It | **DOES** |

### Reponses courtes

| Question | Oui | Non |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Do you like pizza? | Yes, I **do**. | No, I **don't**. |
| Does she play tennis? | Yes, she **does**. | No, she **doesn't**. |

## Les mots interrogatifs

### Les principaux mots

| Mot | Signification | Exemple |
|-----|---------------|---------|
| **What** | Quoi / Que | What is your name? |
| **Where** | Ou | Where do you live? |
| **When** | Quand | When is your birthday? |
| **Who** | Qui | Who is your teacher? |
| **Why** | Pourquoi | Why are you sad? |
| **How** | Comment | How are you? |

### Mots composes avec HOW

| Mot | Signification | Exemple |
|-----|---------------|---------|
| **How old** | Quel age | How old are you? |
| **How many** | Combien (comptable) | How many brothers do you have? |
| **How much** | Combien (non-comptable) | How much is it? |

---

## Structure des questions

### Avec BE

```
Mot interrogatif + BE + sujet + ... ?
```

| Exemple | Traduction |
|---------|------------|
| **What is** your name? | Quel est ton nom ? |
| **Where are** you? | Ou es-tu ? |
| **Who is** this? | Qui est-ce ? |

### Avec DO/DOES

```
Mot interrogatif + DO/DOES + sujet + verbe + ... ?
```

| Exemple | Traduction |
|---------|------------|
| **What do** you like? | Qu'est-ce que tu aimes ? |
| **Where does** she live? | Ou habite-t-elle ? |
| **When do** they play? | Quand jouent-ils ? |

---

## Questions utiles au quotidien

| Question | Traduction |
|----------|------------|
| What time is it? | Quelle heure est-il ? |
| Can I help you? | Puis-je t'aider ? |
| Can I go to the toilet? | Puis-je aller aux toilettes ? |
| Can you repeat, please? | Peux-tu repeter, s'il te plait ? |
| What does it mean? | Qu'est-ce que ca veut dire ? |

??? success "Correction"
    **Does** she like chocolate? (does → verbe sans s)

??? success "Correction"
    **Who** is your teacher?

??? success "Correction"
    **Where** do you live?

    - Questions avec BE : inversion (Are you...? Is she...?)
    - Questions avec DO/DOES : Do you like...? Does she play...?
    - Mots interrogatifs : What, Where, When, Who, Why, How
    - Reponses courtes : Yes, I do / No, I don't
