|---------|----------|

| **sister** | soeur |

| **grandfather / grandpa** | grand-pere / papi |

|---------|----------|

## Les adjectifs possessifs

| Pronom | Possessif | Exemple |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| I | **my** | **My** mother is kind. |
| You | **your** | Is **your** brother tall? |
| He | **his** | **His** father is a teacher. |
| She | **her** | **Her** sister is 5. |
| It | **its** | The cat and **its** toy. |
| We | **our** | **Our** grandmother is nice. |
| They | **their** | **Their** parents are doctors. |

!!! warning "Attention"
    - **his** = a lui (masculin)
    - **her** = a elle (feminin)

---

## Presenter sa famille

### Phrases utiles

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I have a brother. | J'ai un frere. |
| I don't have a sister. | Je n'ai pas de soeur. |
| My mother is a nurse. | Ma mere est infirmiere. |
| My father is 40 years old. | Mon pere a 40 ans. |
| I have two grandmothers. | J'ai deux grands-meres. |

### Decrire les membres de la famille

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| tall | grand |
| short | petit |
| young | jeune |
| old | vieux |
| nice | gentil |
| funny | drole |

---

## Dialogue exemple

!!! example "Parler de sa famille"
    **A**: Do you have brothers or sisters?
    **B**: Yes, I have one brother and one sister.
    **A**: How old are they?
    **B**: My brother is 12 and my sister is 6.
    **A**: What's your mother's job?
    **B**: My mother is a teacher.

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Comment dit-on 'grand-pere' en anglais ?"

??? success "Correction"
    **grandfather** ou **grandpa**

!!! question "Q2. Complete : ___ sister is 10 years old (sa soeur a elle)"

??? success "Correction"
    **Her** sister is 10 years old.

!!! question "Q3. Traduis : 'J'ai deux freres'"

??? success "Correction"
    I have two brothers.

!!! question "Q4. Presente ta famille en 3 phrases"

??? success "Correction"
    Exemple :
    - My mother is [prenom]. She is [age] years old.
    - My father is [prenom]. He is a [metier].
    - I have [nombre] brother(s)/sister(s).

---

    - Famille : mother, father, brother, sister, grandmother, grandfather
    - Possessifs : my, your, his, her, our, their
    - **his** = a lui (masculin), **her** = a elle (feminin)
