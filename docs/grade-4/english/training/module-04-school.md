| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|

### Exemple de journee

!!! example "A typical school day"
    - **8:30** - I go to school.
    - **9:00** - I have Maths.
    - **10:00** - Break time!
    - **10:15** - I have English.
    - **12:00** - Lunch time.
    - **14:00** - I have Art.
    - **16:30** - I go home.

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Comment dit-on 'trousse' en anglais ?"

    **pencil case**

!!! question "Q2. Traduis : 'Ma matiere preferee est les maths'"

    My favourite subject is Maths.

!!! question "Q3. Complete : I ___ (lire) a book."
