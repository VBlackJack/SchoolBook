# Module 8 - Le present simple

!!! abstract "Objectifs"
    - Conjuguer les verbes au present simple
    - Connaitre la regle du -s a la 3e personne
    - Former des phrases affirmatives et negatives

    **Duree** : 1 heure

---

## Le present simple

### Utilisation

Le present simple sert a exprimer :

- Des **habitudes** : I play football every Saturday.
- Des **gouts** : She likes chocolate.
- Des **verites generales** : The sun rises in the east.

---

## Conjugaison

### Forme affirmative

| Pronom | Verbe | Exemple |
|--------|-------|---------|
| I | play | I play tennis. |
| You | play | You play tennis. |
| He | play**s** | He play**s** tennis. |
| She | play**s** | She play**s** tennis. |
| It | play**s** | It play**s**. |
| We | play | We play tennis. |
| They | play | They play tennis. |

!!! warning "La regle du -S"
    A la 3e personne du singulier (**he, she, it**), on ajoute **-s** au verbe !

---

## Les terminaisons speciales

### Verbes en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o

On ajoute **-es** :

| Verbe | He/She/It |
|-------|-----------|
| watch | watch**es** |
| wash | wash**es** |
| go | go**es** |
| do | do**es** |

### Verbes en consonne + y

Le **y** devient **-ies** :

| Verbe | He/She/It |
|-------|-----------|
| study | stud**ies** |
| try | tr**ies** |
| fly | fl**ies** |

!!! note "Exception"
    Si le y est precede d'une voyelle, on ajoute juste -s : play → plays

---

## La forme negative

### Avec DO/DOES + NOT

| Affirmatif | Negatif |
|------------|---------|
| I like pizza | I **don't like** pizza |
| She likes pizza | She **doesn't like** pizza |

!!! warning "Important"
    Avec **doesn't**, le verbe perd son **-s** !
    - She like**s** → She doesn't like

### Contractions

| Forme longue | Contractee |
|--------------|------------|
| do not | **don't** |
| does not | **doesn't** |

## Exprimer ses gouts

### J'aime / Je n'aime pas

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **like** chocolate. | J'aime le chocolat. |
| I **love** pizza! | J'adore la pizza ! |
| I **don't like** fish. | Je n'aime pas le poisson. |
| I **hate** spiders. | Je deteste les araignees. |

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Complete : She (like) ___ music."

??? success "Correction"
    She **likes** music. (3e personne → +s)

!!! question "Q2. Complete : They (watch) ___ TV every day."

??? success "Correction"
    They **watch** TV every day. (they → pas de s)

!!! question "Q3. Mets a la forme negative : He plays football."

??? success "Correction"
    He **doesn't play** football. (doesn't → verbe sans s)

!!! question "Q4. Complete : My brother (go) ___ to school."

??? success "Correction"
    My brother **goes** to school. (go → goes avec he/she/it)

!!! question "Q5. Traduis : 'Je n'aime pas les maths'"

??? success "Correction"
    I **don't like** Maths.

---

!!! summary "Bilan"
    - Present simple : verbe de base (+ **s** a la 3e personne)
    - He/She/It : +s (plays, likes, watches, goes)
    - Negatif : don't / doesn't + verbe de base
    - Gouts : like, love, hate
