## Parler de son animal

### Poser des questions

| Question | Traduction |
|----------|------------|
| **Do you have a pet?** | As-tu un animal de compagnie ? |
| **What pet do you have?** | Quel animal as-tu ? |
| **What's its name?** | Comment s'appelle-t-il ? |
| **How old is it?** | Quel age a-t-il ? |

### Repondre

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| Yes, I have a dog. | Oui, j'ai un chien. |
| No, I don't have a pet. | Non, je n'ai pas d'animal. |
| Its name is Max. | Il s'appelle Max. |
| It is 3 years old. | Il a 3 ans. |

### Adjectifs utiles

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| **big** | grand |
| **small** | petit |

| **slow** | lent |
| **cute** | mignon |
| **dangerous** | dangereux |
| **friendly** | gentil |

### Les parties du corps

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| **head** | tete |
| **eyes** | yeux |
| **ears** | oreilles |
| **nose** | nez |
| **mouth** | bouche |
| **legs** | pattes/jambes |
| **tail** | queue |

### Exemple de description

!!! example "Description"
    My dog is **big** and **brown**.
    He has four **legs** and a long **tail**.
    He is very **friendly** and **funny**.

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Comment dit-on 'lapin' en anglais ?"

??? success "Correction"
    **rabbit**

!!! question "Q2. Traduis : 'J'ai un chat'"

??? success "Correction"
    I have a cat.

!!! question "Q3. Reponds a : 'Do you have a pet?'"

??? success "Correction"
    Yes, I have a [animal]. / No, I don't have a pet.

!!! question "Q4. Decris un animal en 2 phrases"

??? success "Correction"
    Exemple : A lion is big and dangerous. It has four legs and a tail.
