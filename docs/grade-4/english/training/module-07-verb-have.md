# Module 7 - Le verbe HAVE

!!! abstract "Objectifs"
    - Conjuguer le verbe HAVE au present
    - Utiliser les formes affirmative et negative
    - Parler de ce qu'on possede

    **Duree** : 1 heure

---

## Le verbe HAVE (avoir)

### Conjugaison au present

| Pronom | HAVE | Exemple |
|--------|------|---------|
| **I** | **have** | I have a dog. |
| **You** | **have** | You have blue eyes. |
| **He** | **has** | He has a bike. |
| **She** | **has** | She has a sister. |
| **It** | **has** | It has four legs. |
| **We** | **have** | We have a big house. |
| **They** | **have** | They have two cats. |

!!! warning "Attention !"
    **He, She, It** → **has** (pas have !)

---

## Les formes contractees

| Forme longue | Contractee |
|--------------|------------|
| I have | I've |
| You have | You've |
| He has | He's |
| She has | She's |
| We have | We've |
| They have | They've |

!!! note "Remarque"
    "He's" peut signifier "He is" OU "He has" selon le contexte.
    - He's happy = He is happy
    - He's got a car = He has got a car

---

## La forme negative

### Avec DO/DOES + NOT

| Affirmatif | Negatif |
|------------|---------|
| I have a dog | I **don't have** a dog |
| She has a cat | She **doesn't have** a cat |

!!! warning "Attention"
    Avec **doesn't**, le verbe redevient **have** (pas has) !
    - She has → She doesn't have

### Formes contractees negatives

| Forme longue | Contractee |
|--------------|------------|
| do not have | **don't have** |
| does not have | **doesn't have** |

---

## HAVE GOT

En anglais britannique, on utilise souvent **HAVE GOT** :

| Have | Have got |
|------|----------|
| I have a car | I**'ve got** a car |
| She has a dog | She**'s got** a dog |

Le sens est le meme !

### Negatif avec HAVE GOT

| Affirmatif | Negatif |
|------------|---------|
| I've got a bike | I **haven't got** a bike |
| She's got a cat | She **hasn't got** a cat |

## Utilisation de HAVE

### Possessions

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **have** a new phone. | J'ai un nouveau telephone. |
| She **has** many books. | Elle a beaucoup de livres. |

### Famille

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **have** two brothers. | J'ai deux freres. |
| He **has** no sisters. | Il n'a pas de soeur. |

### Description physique

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **have** blue eyes. | J'ai les yeux bleus. |
| She **has** long hair. | Elle a les cheveux longs. |

---

## Poser des questions

### Avec DO/DOES

| Question | Reponse |
|----------|---------|
| **Do** you **have** a pet? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| **Does** she **have** a brother? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |

### Avec HAVE GOT

| Question | Reponse |
|----------|---------|
| **Have** you **got** a pet? | Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. |
| **Has** she **got** a brother? | Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't. |

---

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Complete : She ___ two cats."

??? success "Correction"
    She **has** two cats.

!!! question "Q2. Complete : They ___ a big garden."

??? success "Correction"
    They **have** a big garden.

!!! question "Q3. Mets a la forme negative : He has a bike."

??? success "Correction"
    He **doesn't have** a bike. / He **hasn't got** a bike.

!!! question "Q4. Pose la question : you / have / a dog ?"

??? success "Correction"
    **Do** you **have** a dog? / **Have** you **got** a dog?

!!! question "Q5. Traduis : 'J'ai les cheveux bruns'"

??? success "Correction"
    I **have** brown hair. / I've got brown hair.

---

!!! summary "Bilan"
    - HAVE : I have, you have, **he/she/it has**, we have, they have
    - Negatif : don't have / doesn't have
    - HAVE GOT = HAVE (meme sens)
    - Questions : Do you have...? / Have you got...?
