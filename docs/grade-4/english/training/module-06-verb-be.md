# Module 6 - Le verbe BE

!!! abstract "Objectifs"
    - Conjuguer le verbe BE au present
    - Utiliser les formes affirmative, negative et contractee
    - Construire des phrases avec BE

    **Duree** : 1 heure

---

## Le verbe BE (etre)

Le verbe **BE** est le verbe le plus important en anglais !

### Conjugaison au present

| Pronom | BE | Exemple |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **I** | **am** | I am happy. |
| **You** | **are** | You are nice. |
| **He** | **is** | He is tall. |
| **She** | **is** | She is a student. |
| **It** | **is** | It is a cat. |
| **We** | **are** | We are friends. |
| **They** | **are** | They are at school. |

!!! tip "Astuce"
    - **am** → uniquement avec **I**
    - **is** → avec **he, she, it**
    - **are** → avec **you, we, they**

---

## Les formes contractees

En anglais parle, on utilise souvent les formes **contractees** :

| Forme longue | Forme contractee |
|--------------|------------------|
| I am | **I'm** |
| You are | **You're** |
| He is | **He's** |
| She is | **She's** |
| It is | **It's** |
| We are | **We're** |
| They are | **They're** |

!!! example "Exemples"
    - I'm 10 years old. (= I am 10 years old)
    - She's my sister. (= She is my sister)
    - They're French. (= They are French)

---

## La forme negative

Pour faire une phrase negative, on ajoute **not** apres BE :

| Affirmatif | Negatif long | Negatif contracte |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| I am happy | I am not happy | I'm not happy |
| He is tall | He is not tall | He isn't tall |
| They are here | They are not here | They aren't here |

### Contractions negatives

| Forme longue | Contractee |
|--------------|------------|
| is not | **isn't** |
| are not | **aren't** |

!!! warning "Attention"
    On ne dit PAS "I amn't". On dit "I'm not".

---

## Utilisation de BE

### Pour decrire

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **am** tall. | Je suis grand. |
| She **is** beautiful. | Elle est belle. |
| The cat **is** black. | Le chat est noir. |

### Pour dire l'age

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **am** 10 years old. | J'ai 10 ans. |
| He **is** 8. | Il a 8 ans. |

### Pour dire ou on est

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| I **am** at home. | Je suis a la maison. |
| They **are** at school. | Ils sont a l'ecole. |

### Pour les metiers

| Anglais | Francais |
|---------|----------|
| My mother **is** a doctor. | Ma mere est medecin. |
| They **are** teachers. | Ils sont professeurs. |

## Exercices

!!! question "Q1. Complete : She ___ 9 years old."

??? success "Correction"
    She **is** 9 years old.

!!! question "Q2. Complete : We ___ happy."

??? success "Correction"
    We **are** happy.

!!! question "Q3. Mets a la forme negative : He is at school."

??? success "Correction"
    He **is not** at school. / He **isn't** at school.

!!! question "Q4. Ecris la forme contractee : I am a student."

??? success "Correction"
    **I'm** a student.

!!! question "Q5. Traduis : 'Ils ne sont pas francais'"

??? success "Correction"
    They **are not** French. / They **aren't** French.

---

!!! summary "Bilan"
    - BE = am, is, are
    - **I am** / **You are** / **He is** / **She is** / **We are** / **They are**
    - Contractions : I'm, you're, he's, she's, we're, they're
    - Negatif : + **not** (isn't, aren't)
